Professional FAQ

Do you use poison for mole control on farms?

No, we use only traditional mechanical traps (Duffus & Fenn) – completely poison-free and safe for livestock, wildlife, and the environment. This approach is Red Tractor compliant and environmentally responsible. We believe in sustainable pest management that maintains ecological balance.

What areas do you specialize in for mole control?

We specialize in professional mole control for:

  • Working farms and agricultural land
  • Golf courses and sports fields
  • Large estates and country parks
  • Equestrian centres and paddocks
  • Commercial turf and grassland

We do not service standard residential gardens unless they are acreage properties or part of larger estates.

Are you Red Tractor compliant for farm work?

Yes, all our farm work is fully compliant with Red Tractor standards. We provide complete documentation and audit-ready records, including:

  • Trap location maps and inspection schedules
  • Compliance certificates and audit documentation
  • Risk assessments and method statements
  • Regular progress reports and monitoring data
How do you manage moles on golf courses and sports fields?

We provide strategic mole management programs specifically designed for recreational turf:

  • Regular inspection schedules (weekly/fortnightly/monthly)
  • Discreet trap placement away from play areas
  • Maintenance programs to keep greens, fairways and playing surfaces safe
  • Minimal disruption to daily operations and events
  • Professional consultation on long-term mole prevention strategies
What's your approach to large acreage properties?

For large estates and country parks, we implement comprehensive management programs:

  • Initial assessment and mole activity mapping
  • Strategic trap placement in high-activity areas
  • Regular maintenance visits (frequency based on acreage)
  • Ongoing monitoring and activity reporting
  • Customized solutions for specific land use (grazing, crops, recreation)
