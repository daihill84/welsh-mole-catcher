Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use poison for moles?

No. We only use traditional mechanical traps (Duffus & Fenn) – completely poison-free and safe for wildlife, pets, livestock and the environment.

Is it safe for farms?

Yes. All farm and rural work is fully compliant with Red Tractor standards, including documentation and audit-ready records.

Do you guarantee complete clearance of moles or rats?

On working farms and rural land, complete elimination of all activity is rarely realistic or necessary — nature always finds its own beautiful balance. Our expert goal is to confidently reduce pest numbers to safe, manageable levels, while protecting your livestock, crops, wildlife and the surrounding ecosystem for long-term farm productivity and environmental health. With David’s 30+ years of ex-Pestokil experience, you get highly effective, poison-free solutions that support your farm’s success and Red Tractor compliance.

Do you also control rats?

Yes. We offer poison-free rural rat control using Fenn traps – safe for farms, livestock and wildlife. See our specialist rat control pages on the Neath, Merthyr and Brecon hubs.
