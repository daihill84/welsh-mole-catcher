Why Choose a Traditional Mole Catcher?

Protect Your Silage: Moles contaminate silage with soil, which can lead to Listeriosis in livestock. My traditional trapping methods remove the threat quickly and safely.

Prevent Machinery Damage: Mole hills are more than an eyesore; they destroy mowing blades and expensive agricultural equipment. Professional clearance saves you money.

Poison-Free & Safe: I use mechanical traps (Duffus/Claw) which are 100% poison-free, making them safe for farms with dogs, livestock, and children.
