Professional Mole Control FAQs

What areas of Wales do you cover for professional mole control?

We provide professional mole control services across South Wales and Mid Wales, including:

  • South Wales: Neath Valley, Swansea area, Cardiff rural areas, Merthyr Tydfil, Rhondda Cynon Taf, Blaenau Gwent, Caerphilly
  • Mid Wales: Brecon Beacons, Powys, Carmarthenshire, Pembrokeshire, Monmouthshire
  • Specific Areas: Brecon, Hay-on-Wye, Builth Wells, Llandrindod Wells, Newtown, Welshpool
Do you use poison for mole control on farms?

No, we use only traditional mechanical traps (Duffus & Fenn traps) – completely poison-free and safe for livestock, wildlife, pets, and the environment. This approach is:

  • Red Tractor compliant for farm assurance
  • Environmentally responsible
  • Safe for all non-target species
  • Immediate and effective
Are you Red Tractor compliant for farm work?

Yes, all our farm work is fully compliant with Red Tractor standards. We provide complete documentation including:

  • Trap location maps and inspection schedules
  • Compliance certificates and audit documentation
  • Risk assessments and method statements
  • Regular progress reports and monitoring data
  • Environmental impact assessments
How do you manage moles on golf courses and sports fields?

We provide strategic mole management programs specifically designed for recreational turf:

  • Regular inspection schedules: Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly as required
  • Discreet trap placement: Away from play areas and foot traffic
  • Maintenance programs: To keep greens, fairways and playing surfaces safe
  • Minimal disruption: Work scheduled around events and peak usage times
  • Professional consultation: On long-term mole prevention strategies
What makes traditional mole trapping better than poison?

Traditional trapping offers several key advantages for professional land management:

  • Immediate results: Traps work instantly, no waiting for poison to take effect
  • Targeted control: Only affects moles, no risk to other wildlife
  • No environmental contamination: No chemicals entering the soil or water systems
  • Livestock safety: No risk of secondary poisoning to grazing animals
  • Audit friendly: Easier to document and demonstrate compliance
